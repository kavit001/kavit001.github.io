Privacy policy

Personal Agent App. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who uses it

Personal Agent App is used only by its owner. It isn't offered to the public.

What it can see

With the owner's permission, given at Google's sign-in, it has read-only access to the owner's own:

  • Gmail: email messages and settings
  • Google Calendar: events, and the list of calendars

It can't send, delete, move or change any email or event.

How that data is used

Only to answer the owner's own requests, such as listing unread mail or showing today's agenda. Answers go to the owner's private Telegram chat with the app.

The app doesn't keep copies of emails or events. It keeps Google's IDs for the items it last listed, for an hour, so the owner can ask for "item 3", and a record of the owner's own requests.

Sharing

Google user data isn't sold, shown to anyone other than the owner, used for advertising, or used to develop or train AI models.

Personal Agent App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Ending access

The owner can end the app's access at any time, at Google Account: third-party apps and services.

Changes

If the app's use of Google data changes, this page is updated first.
